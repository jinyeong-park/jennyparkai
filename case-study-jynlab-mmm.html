AI-Assisted Marketing Mix Modeling (MMM) & Forecasting

With third-party cookie deprecation restricting granular user-level tracking, traditional attribution methods fail to account for offline media and long-term channel impacts. This project built a macro-level Bayesian Marketing Mix Model in Python — quantifying carryover effects and saturation points across channels, then layered an LLM-powered copilot to automate budget optimization and executive reporting.

The Problem

Attribution Blind Spots in a Cookie-Less World

Signal Loss & Privacy Barriers: Cookie-less environments prevent deterministic tracking for offline channels (TV, sponsorships) and cross-device digital campaigns — making platform-reported ROAS unreliable for executive budget decisions.

Non-Linear Ad Dynamics: Marketing spend suffers from lag effects (Adstock carryover) and diminishing marginal returns. Linear ROI projections were causing the team to over-invest in saturated channels while under-funding high-potential ones.

Approach

Data Architecture & Analytical Approach

1. Bayesian Time-Series MMM (Python)

Utilized Bayesian statistical modeling to quantify macro-level channel performance over time, capturing dynamics that platform dashboards fundamentally cannot measure:

  • Adstock Transformation (Carryover Effect): Modeled decay rate functions to capture lingering consumer memory effects across TV, Paid Search, Social, and Sponsorships — accounting for the fact that ad exposure impacts behavior days or weeks after the impression.
  • Hill Saturation Function (Diminishing Returns): Mapped non-linear budget curves to identify exact inflection points where incremental spend yields diminishing returns — enabling precise channel-level budget ceilings.

2. AI-Powered Insight Copilot (LLM Pipeline Integration)

  • Built an automated Python pipeline using LLM API integrations (Claude / OpenAI) to process weekly model outputs, generate executive summary memos, and trigger automated alerts when channel budgets reach saturation thresholds.
  • Enabled dynamic budget scenario planning through an interactive forecasting interface for executive decision-making — turning a quarterly analysis into a continuous optimization workflow.

Key Findings

Channel Optimization & Budget Reallocation

The MMM revealed significant misalignment between current spend and optimal budget allocation — Paid Social and Sponsorships were both past their saturation points, while Paid Search and Brand TV remained under-invested.
