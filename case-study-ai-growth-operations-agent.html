The Problem

Cheap Trials Were Masking Expensive, Low-Quality Acquisition.

Wrong Optimization Metric: Trial CAC rewarded cheap signups, even if those owners never launched a campaign. The team needed CPAO: cost per owner who activated by launching a marketing campaign within 14 days.

Creative Fatigue Without a System: Performance was decaying after early creative wins, but there was no consistent rule for separating a weak channel from a tired asset.

AI Without Governance Risked Becoming Noise: A useful agent needed to generate briefs and explanations, but budget recommendations had to remain deterministic, auditable, and human-approved.

Tablr is a fictional B2B SaaS product for independent restaurant owners. The project simulates 12 weeks of paid media, product activation, subscription, and revenue data to test whether a growth team can use AI to move faster without giving up measurement discipline.

Measurement Strategy

CPAO Reframed the Channel Decision.

Trial CAC was treated as context, not the decision metric. The primary metric became Cost Per Activated Owner, defined as spend divided by owners who launched their first Tablr campaign within 14 days of trial signup. That behavioral milestone is much closer to future subscription value than a top-of-funnel signup.

Key Insight
META was not just more expensive at the trial stage. Its CPAO was 4.7x TikTok's, which means it acquired restaurant owners who were harder to activate. Optimizing on trial CAC alone would have kept spend flowing toward the wrong channel.

Experiment Governance

Every Test Had a Primary Metric, Guardrail, and Decision Rule.

The experiment registry prevented the agent from treating every performance lift as a win. A result had to clear both statistical confidence and practical significance before being promoted, and guardrail violations automatically downgraded scale recommendations to hold.

exp_001 - Running
META Hook Type Test
CONTRAST vs OUTCOME for scrappy independent owners. Tests whether the META problem is creative-solvable or channel-structural.
Primary: activation rate
exp_002 - Running
Messaging Territory
SOCIAL_PROOF vs FOMO for growth-minded owners, with CPAO as a guardrail so a click lift cannot hide poor activation.
Guardrail: CPAO
exp_003 - Running
TikTok Format Test
UGC video vs static creative for new owners. CTR is primary, but activation rate prevents optimization for empty clicks.
Guardrail: activation rate
exp_004 - Evaluating
LinkedIn Channel Test
Job-title targeting vs META lookalike for delivery-heavy owners, designed around CPAO and activation quality.
Primary: CPAO
Decision Gate
A test needed 95% confidence and practical significance against the minimum detectable effect before action. The agent could explain the result, but it could not overrule the policy engine.

Architecture

The LLM Could Explain Decisions, Not Make Them.

1
Analytics Engine
Python + DuckDB + Parquet
Computes channel scorecards, creative diagnostics, cohort retention, LTV:CAC, and budget scenarios from reproducible synthetic data.
2
Policy Engine
Deterministic Rules
Sets recommendation states using explicit thresholds: LTV:CAC, M6 maturity, retention guardrails, trial floors, and missing-data checks.
3
LLM Analyst Layer
Narrative Only
Receives the policy state plus evidence dictionary, then writes rationale, tradeoffs, risks, and next experiment hypotheses.
4
Governance Layer
Recommend Only
Writes auditable recommendations and disabled review actions. No ad platform mutation, no budget change, no campaign pause.
Safety Constraint
RECOMMEND_ONLY=True is enforced throughout the system. The output is a decision packet for a human operator, not an autonomous media-buying agent.

Limitations

The Simulation Is Honest About What It Cannot Prove.

  • Synthetic data: All restaurant owners, campaigns, spend, revenue, and outcomes are simulated for portfolio demonstration.
  • Short observation window: The 12-week period is not long enough for mature M6 retention on newer cohorts.
  • No live platform mutations: The project does not call Meta Ads, TikTok, Google Ads, or LinkedIn campaign APIs to spend money or alter campaigns.
  • Mock analyst layer: The LLM-style narrative layer is a controlled provider pattern, not a production deployment with full prompt evaluation.
  • Attribution remains imperfect: UNKNOWN source records are preserved as a visible data quality issue, not forcibly reassigned.
