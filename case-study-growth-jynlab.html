Multi-Touch Attribution & Incrementality Analytics

The Problem

Platform Numbers Didn't Add Up

Attribution Overlap: Meta, Google, and TikTok each reported conversions using their own attribution windows. The same customer journey could therefore be credited to multiple platforms, overstating aggregate marketing performance.

Demand Capture vs. Demand Creation: Meta Retargeting reported strong ROAS, but platform attribution alone could not determine whether those conversions were actually caused by advertising or would have occurred through Organic, Direct, or another marketing channel.

The core business problem was not simply determining which channel received credit for a conversion. It was determining: Which channels actually created additional customer demand?

Approach

From Raw Touchpoints to Marketing Decisions

1. Unified Marketing Data Pipeline

The analysis combines marketing spend, user touchpoints, campaign metadata, and backend conversion records into a unified analytical dataset.

  • Ad Spend Data: Campaign spend and platform-reported metrics from Meta, Google Search, and TikTok.
  • User Touchpoint Logs: Clickstream events containing user IDs, timestamps, channels, campaigns, and UTM parameters.
  • Backend Conversions: Ground-truth purchase records used to validate conversion events independently of ad platform claims.

2. Customer Journey Reconstruction

Before assigning attribution, customer interactions were reconstructed chronologically to understand how users moved across channels before converting.

PostgreSQL window functions and CTEs were used to sequence touchpoints, identify first and last interactions, and calculate each channel's position within the customer journey.

3. SQL Multi-Touch Attribution

Built a SQL-based attribution layer using PostgreSQL window functions, CTEs, and customer journey sequencing to compare five attribution models side-by-side.

  • First-Touch: Credits the channel responsible for customer discovery.
  • Last-Touch: Credits the final interaction before conversion.
  • Linear: Distributes credit equally across all touchpoints.
  • Time-Decay: Assigns greater credit to interactions closer to conversion.
  • Position-Based: Assigns greater weight to the first and last touchpoints while distributing the remaining credit across middle interactions.

4. Geo-Holdout Incrementality Testing

Attribution models estimate which channels should receive credit. Incrementality testing answers a different question:

Would this conversion have happened without the marketing intervention?

  • Conducted a 30-day matched-market geo-holdout experiment across treatment and control DMAs.
  • Compared conversion outcomes between markets where campaigns remained active and matched markets where campaigns were held out.
  • Estimated Incremental Conversions, Conversion Lift, Incremental CAC, and Incremental ROAS.

Attribution models consistently showed that Meta captured credit late in the customer journey, while geo-holdout testing suggested that a meaningful portion of those conversions may have occurred without Meta exposure.

TikTok showed the opposite pattern: weaker last-touch performance but stronger first-touch and incremental performance. This suggested that TikTok was more effective at creating demand than capturing existing demand.

Recommendation

From Measurement to Budget Decisions

The goal of the analysis was not simply to compare metrics. The final step was translating measurement results into clear marketing decisions.

Meta Paid Social

High Platform ROAS → Low Incremental ROAS → Reduce Retargeting Spend

Meta Retargeting appeared to capture conversions generated by other channels and organic demand. The recommendation was to reduce spend in saturated retargeting segments and prioritize higher-value audiences.

Google Paid Search

Strong Last-Touch + Strong Incremental ROAS → Increase High-Intent Budget

Google Search showed strong performance near conversion and maintained high incremental efficiency, supporting additional investment in high-intent campaigns.

TikTok Ads

Weak Last-Touch + Strong First-Touch + Strong Incrementality → Increase Prospecting Investment

TikTok was undervalued by last-touch attribution but demonstrated stronger top-of-funnel contribution and incremental impact, supporting increased investment in prospecting campaigns.
