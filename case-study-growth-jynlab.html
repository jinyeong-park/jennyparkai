Multi-Touch Attribution & Geo-Incrementality Engine

Modern ad platforms over-report conversion metrics due to self-attribution bias, overlapping tracking windows, and signal loss post-iOS 14. This project built an end-to-end analytics engine that unifies raw ad spend with deterministic clickstream event logs to uncover true campaign ROI, reallocate marketing budgets, and optimize customer acquisition costs.

The Problem

Platform Numbers Didn't Add Up

Self-Attribution Overlap: Platform-reported conversions over-estimated combined paid media ROI by 34% due to duplicate attribution claims across Meta Retargeting and Google Search.

Wasted Spend: Meta Retargeting claimed high ROAS (3.25x), but much of this conversion volume was cannibalizing users who would have converted naturally via Organic or Direct channels.

Approach

Data Architecture & Analytical Approach

1. Data Pipeline & Ingestion

  • Ad Spend Data (`raw_ad_spend`): Ingested API spend and platform-reported metrics from Meta, Google, and TikTok across Designated Market Areas (DMAs).
  • Clickstream Logs (`raw_user_touchpoints`): Extracted granular user session logs (GA4 / CDP event streaming) capturing user_id, UTM parameters, timestamps, and location data.
  • Backend Transactions (`raw_conversions`): Joined ground-truth purchase records from backend database logs to validate true revenue independent of ad pixel claims.

2. SQL Multi-Touch Attribution (MTA) Engine

Utilized PostgreSQL window functions (ROW_NUMBER, FIRST_VALUE, LAG) and CTEs to model user conversion touchpoint sequences across three attribution models:

  • First-Touch: Identified discovery-driven channels — TikTok Ads emerged as a top-of-funnel engine.
  • Last-Touch: Captured intent-driven conversion endpoints — Google Paid Search.
  • Time-Decay: Applied an exponential decay formula with a 7-day half-life to distribute revenue credit across multi-channel customer journeys.

3. Python Matched-Market Geo-Incrementality Testing

  • Conducted a 30-day Geo-Holdout Experiment isolating 50 DMAs into Treatment (Meta Ads active) and Control (Meta Ads paused) groups.
  • Calculated baseline organic retention and isolated true Incremental Cost Per Acquisition (iCAC) and Incremental ROAS (iROAS).

Key Findings

Cross-Channel Performance Reality

Comparing platform-reported ROAS against SQL MTA models and geo-incrementality results revealed significant divergence — and a clear path to budget reallocation.
