# Metric Definitions

All core lifecycle metrics are calculated at the organization (account/workspace) grain unless noted otherwise. The source data is synthetic and deterministic.

| Metric | Numerator | Denominator | Grain | Caveat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Signup | Organizations with a `created_at` date in the selected period | None; reported as a count | Organization | Represents an account-level signup/cohort, not every individual user signup. |
| Workspace creation rate | Organizations with a `workspace_created` event | Signup organizations | Organization | Workspace creation is an onboarding milestone, not activation. |
| Workspace→Integration rate | Organizations with an `integration_connected` event (any time, not gated to 7 days) | Organizations with a `workspace_created` event | Organization | Step-to-step rate — conditioned on the prior funnel step, not total signups, so it isolates the integration step's own leak from the workspace step's. Used to distinguish an onboarding/UX friction problem from an acquisition-quality problem when segmented by channel. |
| Activation within 7 days | Organizations with `workspace_created` plus at least one of `teammate_invited`, `integration_connected`, or `project_created` within 7 days of signup | Signup organizations | Organization | Measures a defined early-value proxy; it does not prove long-term value. |
| First-action retention rate | 60-day retention (eligible accounts only) grouped by whichever key action an org completed **first** — not whether it eventually completed all of them | Organizations eligible for the 60-day retention horizon, split by `first_action` | Organization | `milestone_retention()` in `app/utils/metrics.py`. A milestone can look like "the strongest signal" on a naive average that includes ineligible/never-paid orgs as failures; gating to eligible accounts changes the ranking — see `docs/process/05_analysis_findings.md`. |
| P50/P90 days-to-first-action | Days from signup to an org's first `teammate_invited` / `integration_connected` / `project_created` event, unbounded by the 7-day activation window | None (a distribution, not a rate) | Organization | Median/90th percentile, not the mean — a handful of orgs that act months after signup would otherwise inflate the average. `first_key_action()` + `time_to_activate_trend()` / `activation_speed_by_segment()` in `app/utils/metrics.py`. |
| Trial start rate | Organizations with a subscription record | Eligible signup organizations | Organization | In the synthetic model, a subscription record is used as the trial-start indicator; no separate trial event is available. |
| Ever-paid conversion rate | Organizations with a positive-MRR subscription record (`ever_paid`/legacy `paid_customer`) | Signup organizations, unless explicitly filtered to trial starters | Organization | This is a historical conversion fact and remains true after churn. |
| Current paid customers | Organizations with a positive-MRR subscription whose status is active at the shared observation date | Signup organizations, when displayed as a rate | Organization | Stored separately as `current_active_customer`; churned and trial-only organizations are excluded. |
| Retention at 30/60/90 days | Ever-paid organizations whose subscription remains active through the selected tenure horizon | Ever-paid organizations whose paid-conversion date is old enough to reach the horizon by the shared observation date | Organization | Excludes immature account-horizon observations. Signup-month charts group these paid-retention outcomes by acquisition cohort but keep paid conversion as the retention clock. This is **paid/logo retention** — see the next row for the separate engagement-retention cut. |
| Engagement retention at D30/D60/D90 | Organizations with any product event in the day-1–30 / 31–60 / 61–90 window after signup | All signup organizations old enough to have reached the horizon (signup-date clock, not paid-conversion date) | Organization | `engagement_retention_curve()` in `app/utils/metrics.py`, `sql/marts/mart_engagement_retention.sql`. Answers "are they still using it?" across every signup, not just paid accounts — a materially different (lower) number than paid retention, since most signups never convert to paid. Day 0 excluded: signup-day activity is onboarding, not a return visit. |
| Modeled NRR | MRR retained through the selected horizon among mature subscriptions in a subscription-start cohort | Beginning MRR from subscriptions eligible for that cohort horizon | Subscription-start cohort | Immature subscription-horizon observations are omitted. No expansion or contraction events are modeled, so this is a retained-MRR proxy rather than production NRR. |
| Monthly churn (LTV input) | Churn events among paid subscriptions | Account-months of exposure (active subs count as censored exposure up to the observation date) | Paid subscription | `channel_ltv()` in `app/utils/metrics.py`; `sql/marts/mart_revenue.sql`. Not the cumulative churned share (`churned / accounts`, ~21% here vs. ~2.7% monthly), which mixes an entire observation window into one number. |
| LTV (uncapped / capped) | ARPA / monthly churn; capped: ARPA x (1 - (1-c)^H) / c over a fixed horizon H (default 24 months) | None (currency) | Segment | Uncapped assumes constant churn and an unbounded lifetime; the implied ~35-40 months exceeds the ~15 months observed, so prefer capped. Churn here is front-loaded (all in months 2-4 after paying), so even the capped figure understates 24-month LTV by ~9% versus a survival-curve estimate. Intervals: bootstrap over paid accounts (ARPA and churn together). |
| Churn hazard by month since paid start | Churned accounts in that month of paid tenure | Accounts at risk that month (observed for the whole month, or churned in it) | Paid subscription | `paid_survival_curve()` in `app/utils/metrics.py`; Churn Risk page. Still-active subscriptions are censored, and partially observed months don't dilute the rate. Here it is 0.4%/6.2%/6.6%/9.9% in months 1-4 and 0% after, which contradicts the constant-churn assumption in `LTV = ARPA / churn`. |
| Prospective 90-day churn by engagement | Paying accounts at a month-end cutoff that churn in the next 90 days | Paying accounts at that cutoff (cutoffs with an unobserved horizon are dropped) | Account-cutoff (accounts repeat across cutoffs) | `prospective_engagement_dataset()` / `churn_by_feature()`. Features use only events before the cutoff. Always stratify by tenure; unadjusted, recent activity looks harmful because new accounts are both active and early-life. |
| Engagement tier (power / active / at-risk / dormant) | From weekly usage over the last 4 full weeks (recent) and the 4 before (prior): dormant = no recent sessions; at-risk = prior sessions >= 12 and recent <= 50% of prior; power = recent sessions in the top 20% of the paying population and mean features used >= 3; active = the rest (precedence dormant > at-risk > power > active) | Paying accounts at a cutoff | Account-week | `assign_engagement_tiers()`, `sql/marts/mart_usage_engagement.sql`. Uses only weeks that ended before the cutoff. The power cut is relative to the population, so tiers never see churn. Table is synthetic with a planted signal (see README). |
| AUC | Probability that a random churner scores higher than a random non-churner | Churners x non-churners | Account-week | `auc_score()`. 0.5 = no signal. Rank-based, ties averaged. Observations repeat accounts across weekly cutoffs. |
| Max affordable CAC | Capped LTV / target LTV:CAC ratio | None (currency) | Segment | A budget ceiling, not a CAC. No acquisition-spend data exists, so CAC and LTV:CAC are not computed. |
| Current MRR | Sum of positive MRR from subscriptions active at the shared observation date | None; reported as currency | Organization | `mrr_amount` preserves the latest historical subscription value; `current_mrr` is zero for churned, trial-only, and non-subscribed organizations. |
| Current ARPA | Current MRR | Number of current paid customers | Organization | Based on point-in-time synthetic MRR and not adjusted for discounts, usage, or period averages. |
| Churn risk | No single numerator; a 0-100 rules-based score from subscription state, activation, eligible 60-day retention, and activity recency | None | Organization | Immature accounts receive no failed-retention penalty. MRR at risk uses current MRR only. This remains a transparent prioritization heuristic, not a trained predictive model. |
| Experiment lift | Treatment metric rate minus control metric rate | Control metric rate for percent lift, or no denominator for percentage-point lift | Organization assignment | Primary dashboard lift is activation-rate difference in percentage points; causal interpretation assumes valid assignment and instrumentation. |
| Required sample size (N per variant) | `(z_alpha + z_power)^2 * (p1(1-p1) + p2(1-p2)) / (p1-p2)^2`, evaluated at a pre-specified minimum detectable effect (MDE) | None; a planning threshold, not a rate | Organization assignment | `required_sample_size()` in `app/utils/metrics.py`, `sql/marts/mart_experiment.sql` and `mart_multivariate.sql`. MDE must be decided in advance, not set to the observed lift — a null result has ~0 observed lift, which would make every null result look under-powered by construction. |
| Experiment decision (SHIP / ITERATE / CONTINUE / KILL) | Primary-metric significance + guardrail significance (each guardrail gets its own two-proportion test, not a fixed point-estimate cutoff) + sample-size adequacy | None; a synthesized verdict | Organization assignment | `experiment_decision()` in `app/utils/metrics.py`. A guardrail only counts as "broken" if treatment is *significantly* worse than control on that metric — a small negative delta on a small eligible sample is often noise. |
| Rate / mix / interaction effect | Per segment: `(cur_rate - prior_rate) x prior_mix`, `(cur_mix - prior_mix) x (prior_rate - prior_overall_rate)`, and `(cur_mix - prior_mix) x (cur_rate - prior_rate)` | None (contributions in rate points; they sum to the overall rate change) | Organization, by signup month | `mix_rate_decomposition()` / `summarize_decomposition()` in `app/utils/metrics.py`; `sql/marts/mart_root_cause.sql`. The mix effect is centered on the prior overall rate: same total as the uncentered `Δmix x prior_rate`, but per segment it is negative exactly when a segment gained share while activating below average (uncentered, a low-rate channel that grows still shows a positive number). A segment present in only one period is treated as pure mix (its missing rate is filled with the observed one). Attributes a change but doesn't test it: check the change itself is significant first, and treat the largest of many period-over-period moves as a multiple-comparisons pick. Monthly grain; weekly cohorts (~40-50 accounts) are too noisy. |
| Bonferroni-corrected significance | `\|z\| >= z_threshold`, where `z_threshold` is the two-tailed critical value at `alpha / k` (k = number of treatment variants compared to control) | None | Organization assignment, illustrative only | `bonferroni_pairwise_results()` / `simulate_multivariate_variants()` in `app/utils/metrics.py`, `sql/marts/mart_multivariate.sql`. This project's real experiment data only has 2 arms (control/treatment); the 3-way split used here is a stable-hash simulation for demonstrating the multiple-comparisons correction, not a genuine multivariate result. Running k independent tests at alpha=0.05 each inflates the family-wise false-positive rate to `1-(1-alpha)^k`. |

## Revenue Labeling

`Indexed Active MRR` is the overview trend: month-end active MRR snapshots indexed to the first observed month. `Modeled NRR (no expansion events)` appears only on the Revenue page, excludes immature horizon observations, and remains labeled as a synthetic retained-MRR proxy.

## Shared Observation Date

Activity recency, current subscription state, retention eligibility, active-MRR snapshots, and modeled NRR maturity all use the latest timestamp in the deterministic source tables. This prevents pages from drifting onto different as-of dates.
