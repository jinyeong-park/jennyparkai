# Dashboard Story

## Stakeholder Narrative

The dashboard starts with the account lifecycle: how many accounts sign up, create a workspace, activate, start a trial, convert to paid, and remain retained at 60 days. It then ties early product behavior to experiment performance, revenue movement, and a prioritized churn-risk view.

## Reading Order

1. Use the executive overview to identify where the lifecycle leaks.
2. Use Activation to inspect the first-week behaviors associated with value.
3. Use Retention to compare cohort and segment durability.
4. Use Experiments to evaluate whether guided onboarding improves activation and downstream outcomes.
5. Use Revenue to connect the lift to paid MRR and modeled NRR limitations.
6. Use Churn Risk to prioritize transparent, account-level customer-success follow-up.

## Delivery Format

The first interactive artifact is a local Streamlit app, styled after the supplied product-led retention analytics reference. The surrounding case-study documentation remains Hex-oriented so the project can be discussed as a stakeholder-ready analytics application in interviews.
