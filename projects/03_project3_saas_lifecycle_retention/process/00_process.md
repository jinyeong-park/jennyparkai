# Case Study Process

## Product Question

Which early product behaviors help B2B SaaS accounts activate, convert to paid, and retain long enough to create durable recurring revenue?

## Workflow

```text
Define product question -> generate synthetic lifecycle data -> build account metrics -> analyze activation/retention/experiment results -> create stakeholder dashboard -> recommend rollout and measurement plan.
```

## Analyst Deliverables

- A deterministic synthetic dataset that makes the lifecycle logic reproducible.
- Account-level lifecycle metrics for activation, paid conversion, retention, revenue, and churn risk.
- A local Streamlit dashboard for stakeholder exploration.
- Hex-oriented documentation that positions the analysis as a product and business analytics workflow.
- A rollout recommendation with a 60-day retention measurement plan.

## Interview Positioning

This case study demonstrates how a product analyst can connect event instrumentation, lifecycle metrics, experiment analysis, and a stakeholder-facing app to a business decision. It is designed to support discussion of metric definitions, segmentation, experimentation, and the limits of modeled data.
