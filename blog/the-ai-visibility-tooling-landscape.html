Two Ecosystems, One Stack

Why the tooling split matters for how you evaluate vendors

Traditional SEO and ASO platforms were built around a stable, queryable object — a URL's rank, an app's keyword position. GEO monitoring tools were built around something fundamentally less stable: a language model's synthesized answer, which changes across runs and across model versions. Evaluating a GEO vendor with the same checklist you'd use for an SEO suite — keyword coverage, update frequency — misses what actually differentiates them: sampling methodology and source-mix transparency.

GEO / AI Visibility Monitoring

The category that didn't exist three years ago

At the enterprise end, Profound and Scrunch AI run deep multi-engine prompt simulations with competitive displacement tracking and, for Scrunch, SOC 2 compliance for larger org requirements. At the accessible end, Peec AI and Otterly.ai track fixed prompt matrices across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude at a price point that makes sense for a single-product team rather than a platform. Existing SEO suites have folded in their own add-ons — Ahrefs Brand Radar and Semrush AI Toolkit — which is the pragmatic choice if a team is already paying for that suite's keyword and backlink data and doesn't want a second login.

The native console tools — App Store Connect and Google Play Console — deserve a specific mention: they're free, first-party, and the only place Android Vitals thresholds and Custom Product Page management actually live. No enterprise ASO suite replaces them; the paid tools add competitive intelligence on top, not core functionality.

The Selection Question That Matters Most

Buy for the warehouse you'll need in a year, not the dashboard you need today

The decision I'd weight most heavily isn't which GEO tracker has the prettiest UI — it's whether the vendor's data can land in a warehouse (BigQuery, Snowflake, or even DuckDB for a smaller team) alongside GA4 and App Store Console exports. Cross-surface LTV — the number that actually justifies GEO and ASO budget to a CFO — only exists once prompt-level citation data, session data, and store performance data share a join key. A GEO tool with no export path is a dashboard, not infrastructure.
